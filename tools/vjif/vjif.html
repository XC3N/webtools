Fade one-shot: the GIF stays on its layer but is hidden (a dotted ghost in the preview, nothing in the output) until a hit fades it in. Lengths follow the tempo.

X, Y and Rotate are added to the placement; Zoom, Width, Height, Saturation and Brightness multiply it; Opacity multiplies the sprite's opacity; Hue is added. Touching a setting switches its LFO on.

Theme

Signal marks what is selected or on; LCD colours the readouts; Graphs the fade and automation graphs. Theme files are shared with Bad MOPHO (it also uses colours VJif doesn't). The output window always stays black.

Keys & mouse

Loading
Drop GIFs or links onto a pad, or anywhere in the pads panel to fill empty pads; click an empty pad to pick from the GIF pool (or browse, while the pool is empty); Pool lists every GIF of the set; drop a .vjif anywhere to import a set
QWE ASD ZXC · RTY FGH VBN
Trigger a pad: restarts the GIF on whichever layer it's on; if it's on none, adds it to the edited layer (Stack, up to 6 — a full layer flashes red and refuses) or swaps it in (Replace). With Snap on, a hit waits for the next beat / bar (the pad blinks); a hit just after one counts as on it. Fade one-shot GIFs (GIF › Trigger) fade in and out on each hit
Shift+pad key
Select that pad without playing it (same as right-click). Remove a GIF from its layer with Del or the × on its chip
F1–F12
Screen effects on the whole output — Mono · Colour · Strobe · Poster / Zoom · Shake · Wobble · Mirror / Glitch · RGB · Pixel · Feedback, each with a few styles (e.g. Glitch: slices, blocks, melt · RGB: split, VHS · Feedback: trails, tunnel, spiral). Each is Hold (while held), Hit (plays once, fades over Length; with Snap it waits for the beat / bar) or Latch (on / off); Rate sets how fast the moving ones move, in beats. Right-click or Shift+F-key edits one without playing it
0 · Ins
Hold 0: blackout (fades the output to black) · hold Ins: freeze the output on the current frame (effects still play over it)
Numpad 1–9
Go to scene 1–9 (with Snap, on the next beat / bar; the tiles are laid out like the numpad). A scene is its own 18 pads (GIF settings included) and what the four layers hold; a new scene starts empty (Duplicate copies one); a set has nine
Caps Lock + numpad 1–9
Effect presets instead of scenes (the scene tiles dim and the preset row lights up). Each plays a stored mix of effects at once with its own Hold / Hit / Latch, Length and Amount. To store one, switch on the effects you want (hold their F keys, or latch them) and press Shift+numpad key — or right-click the preset and press Store
1–9
Arm a transition preset for the next scene change (the Transition panel edits the armed one)
Shift+1–4
Layer on / off — 1 Background · 2 Midground · 3 Foreground · 4 Overlay
PgUp / PgDn · End
Edit layer up / down (or click its card; Alt+1–4 too) · End: the edit layer on / off
Space
Tap tempo (first tap = downbeat)
Wheel on BPM
Steps the digit under the cursor (hundreds … hundredths, it's underlined)
Shift+Space
Resync: now is beat 1, every GIF restarts
Arrows · Home
Nudge the selected GIF 1 px (Shift = 10 px; with the mouse over a slider or button row, the arrows adjust that control) · Home resets its placement
Sliders
Double-click the bar resets to default · mouse wheel (once the slider glows) or arrows adjust: Up/Down 1 step, Left/Right 10, Shift ×10 · double-click the number to type a value (Enter applies, Esc cancels) · dropdowns and button rows take the wheel and arrows too
Del / ⌫
Remove the selected GIF from its layer
Ctrl+Z
Undo (Ctrl+Shift+Z / Ctrl+Y redo) — moves, resizes, crops, layer and GIF settings
Ctrl+S
Save the current set (first time: name it in Sets)
Esc
Cancel a colour pick · close a panel
Click
Select the topmost visible GIF (Alt+click = the one beneath)
Drag
Move · side handles stretch X/Y · corners zoom (Alt = from center) · Shift+handle crops · round knob rotates (Shift snaps 15°)
Wheel
Zoom · Shift+wheel rotates · double-click resets
Scene tiles
Click to go there · right-click to name it · drag one onto another to swap them
Layer chips
One per GIF on the layer (its pad key). Click to select · drag to another layer or to reorder — the leftmost is drawn on top; a bar shows where it will land · × on hover removes it

GIF pool

Every GIF of this set, decoded once. Click one to put it on the next empty pad of this scene, or drag it onto a pad (replaces what's there; Ctrl+Z undoes). Each scene's pads have their own settings. Drop files or links here to add them without using a pad. Drag the title to move this window.

Sets

Sets are kept in this browser; GIF files are stored once and shared between sets. A .vjif file holds a set plus its GIFs (it's a ZIP) — use it to back up or move a set. Drop a .vjif anywhere on VJif to import it. Loading keeps the current set playing until the new one is decoded, then swaps on the next beat or bar if Snap is set.
