VJifGIF VJ tool · actually, it's pronounced vjif

THIS IS A PREVIEW VERSION, ACT ACCORDINGLY

Play GIFs to the beat: load them onto pads, trigger them with the keyboard, stack them on four layers, switch scenes with transitions and throw effects over it all. Everything runs in this browser tab — nothing is uploaded.

  1. Load GIFs. Drop GIF / WebP / APNG files or links onto a pad (or anywhere in the Pads panel), or click an empty pad. Every GIF you load also goes into the set's Pool.
  2. Play them. Press a pad's key — QWE ASD ZXC and RTY FGH VBN. It lands on the layer you're editing (the framed one). PgUp PgDn change that layer, Shift+1–4 turn layers on and off.
  3. Arrange. In the preview: drag to move, side handles stretch, corners zoom, the round knob rotates, Shift+handle crops, the wheel zooms. Fine-tune in Transform; timing, colour and automation are under GIF.
  4. Keep time. Tap the tempo with Space (or use MIDI clock). With Snap on, hits land on the beat or bar.
  5. Scenes. Num 1–9 switch between nine scenes, each with its own pads and layers. Keys 1–9 pick the transition used for the next switch.
  6. Effects. F1–F12 play screen effects; with Caps Lock on, the numpad plays effect presets. Hold 0 for blackout, Ins to freeze.
  7. Go live. Click Output, move that window to the projector or second screen, double-click it for full screen. Enter starts Prep: the output stays locked on the live scene while you get the next one ready; Enter again sends it out.
  8. Save. Ctrl+S saves the set in this browser; Sets exports it as a .vjif file. Every key and mouse move is listed under ?.

Launchpad map

Launchpad Pro MK3 in Programmer mode. The X and the Mini MK3 only have the top row and the right-hand column of buttons. Hover a pad for its full name; Settings › MIDI control › Launchpad layout fills it, Learn changes any of it.

Keys & mouse

Loading
Drop GIFs or links onto a pad, or anywhere in the pads panel to fill empty pads; click an empty pad to pick from the GIF pool (or browse, while the pool is empty); Pool lists every GIF of the set; drop a .vjif anywhere to import a set
QWE ASD ZXC · RTY FGH VBN
Trigger a pad: restarts the GIF on whichever layer it's on; if it's on none, adds it to the edited layer (up to 6 — a full layer flashes red and refuses). With Snap on, a hit waits for the next beat / bar (the pad blinks); a hit just after one counts as on it. Fade one-shot GIFs (GIF › Trigger) fade in and out on each hit
Alt+pad key · Alt+click
Swap: the GIF takes its layer for itself — the others there go, and it takes over the selected one's placement (pads get a frame while Alt is held). If a Chrome extension uses the same Alt shortcut, the extension wins: change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts
Shift+pad key
Select that pad without playing it (same as right-click). Remove a GIF from its layer with Del or the × on its chip
F1–F12
Screen effects, on the whole output or on one layer (All / 1–4 in the effect settings) — Mono · Colour · Strobe · Poster / Zoom · Shake · Wobble · Mirror / Glitch · CRT · Pixel · Feedback, each with a few styles (e.g. Glitch: slices, blocks, melt, scramble · CRT: split, VHS, scanlines, phosphor, degauss · Feedback: trails, tunnel, spiral). Each is Hold (while held), Hit (with Snap it waits for the beat / bar) or Latch (on / off), shaped by its envelope (Env: drag the points): Attack rises to full, Decay falls to Sustain, a Hit stays there for Length, then Release fades it out (Hold and Latch fade out over Release when let go / switched off); Rate sets how fast the moving ones move, in beats. Right-click or Shift+F-key edits one without playing it
F-key held + 1–4 / 5
While you hold an effect's F-key, 1–4 put layers in or out of the layers it works on, and 5 switches it to All (5 again: back to the layers it had)
0 · Ins
Hold 0: blackout (fades the output to black) · hold Ins: freeze the output on the current frame (effects still play over it)
Numpad 1–9
Go to scene 1–9 (with Snap, on the next beat / bar; the tiles are laid out like the numpad). A scene is its own 18 pads (GIF settings included) and what the four layers hold; a new scene starts empty (Duplicate copies one); a set has nine
Guides, Stick, Ghosts
Under the preview: guide lines over the preview (thirds, golden ratio, centre, or a grid of the columns × rows next to it; click again to hide; preview only), Stick (magnet: a dragged GIF's edges or centre stick to the guides, the centre and the frame edges; hold Alt to place freely) and Ghosts (show or hide faded one-shot GIFs while they're invisible)
Drag a pad onto the preview
Its GIF goes on the edit layer, centred where you drop it
Music & tab audio
Settings › Recording › Music: load a track (or drop an audio file on Settings, or paste a direct link) to play along; with Sound set to "Music player" the recording takes it straight from the file, and With Rec starts it from the top when the take starts (on the bar) and stops it with the take. Sound "Tab audio": when you press Rec, Chrome asks which tab to share (YouTube in another tab works; tick "Also share tab audio") and its sound is recorded with the video
Text…
Lettering (button next to Add…): type a text, pick a font, size, spacing and colour, and it goes on the next empty pad as a one-frame "GIF". Letters: just the text. Cut-out: a matte with see-through letters, to put on a layer above others so they show through the text. Marquee: scrolls across from right to left, one pass per 1–16 bars from the hit (X automation in the Auto tab). Edit text (GIF panel) changes it and keeps the pad's settings. Texts are redrawn when the canvas format changes and saved with the set
BRB
Under the scenes: VJif changes scene by itself every 4 / 8 / 16 / 32 bars, on the bar, to the next scene with GIFs playing (In order) or a random one (Shuffle), with the armed transition or a random preset (Random). The button counts the bars down. Any scene key or click, or BRB again, takes back control; Prep pauses it
Caps Lock + numpad 1–9
Effect presets instead of scenes (the scene tiles dim and the preset row lights up). Each plays a stored mix of effects at once with its own Hold / Hit / Latch, Length, Release and Amount. To fill one: drag effect tiles onto it, or hold its key (Caps on) or its tile and click / press effects to add or remove them; Caps+Shift+numpad key stores the effects that are on right now. Right-click a preset to see and remove its effects
1–9
Arm a transition preset for the next scene change (the Transition panel edits the armed one)
Shift+1–4
Layer on / off — 1 Background · 2 Midground · 3 Foreground · 4 Overlay
PgUp / PgDn · End
Edit layer up / down (or click its card; Alt+1–4 too) · End: the edit layer on / off
Space
Tap tempo (first tap = downbeat)
BPM
Drag up / down or use the wheel over a digit to step that digit (hundreds … hundredths, it's underlined) · click to type a tempo
Shift+Space
Resync: now is beat 1, every GIF restarts
Arrows · Home
Nudge the selected GIF 1 px (Shift = 10 px; with the mouse over a slider or button row, the arrows adjust that control) · Home resets its placement
Sliders
Double-click the bar resets to default · mouse wheel (once the slider glows) or arrows adjust: Up/Down 1 step, Left/Right 10, Shift ×10 · double-click the number to type a value (Enter applies, Esc cancels) · dropdowns and button rows take the wheel and arrows too
Del / ⌫
Remove the selected GIF from its layer
Ctrl+Z
Undo (Ctrl+Shift+Z / Ctrl+Y redo) — moves, resizes, crops, layer and GIF settings
Enter
Prep: the output keeps playing the live scene, untouched, while you work on any scene here (switch scenes, load and place GIFs, try effects: the preview shows it, the output doesn't). The live scene's tile says LIVE. Enter again (or the Prep button) goes live: the scene you're on goes to the output with the armed transition, on the next beat / bar with Snap. Blackout, freeze and recording still act on the output
Output
Opens the output window, where you left it last time (position and size are remembered). Drag anywhere in it to move it; double-click switches full screen on and off. Shift+click Output resets its position if it ends up somewhere unreachable
Rec
Records the output to an MP4 (H.264) or WebM video — Settings › Recording picks which, and the size follows the canvas format. A length in bars (4–32) starts on the next bar and stops exactly on the bar, so the clip loops cleanly (good for Reels). Settings › Recording › Sound records an audio input with it (your interface, or a loopback / virtual cable carrying the mix). Chrome asks where to save first and writes while recording (long sets don't fill memory). With Snap on it starts on the next bar; click again to stop
MIDI learn
Settings › MIDI control › Learn: click a target (pad, scene, transition, effect, layer chip, opacity, effect amount, Tap, Sync, or Blackout/Freeze in the bar), then move a control on your controller. Faders need a knob/fader (CC). Esc or Done to finish; mappings are remembered
Ctrl+S
Save the current set (first time: name it in Sets)
Esc
Cancel a colour pick · close a panel
Click
Select the topmost visible GIF (Alt+click = the one beneath)
Drag
Move · side handles stretch X/Y · corners zoom (Alt = from centre) · Shift+handle crops · round knob rotates (Shift snaps 15°)
Wheel
Zoom · Shift+wheel rotates · double-click resets
Dragging a pad
Onto another pad: they swap (onto an empty one: it moves there) — what plays keeps playing under its new key · onto a layer card: the GIF goes on that layer (on top), or starts there if it wasn't playing. Undoable
Scene tiles
Click to go there · right-click to name it · drag one onto another to swap them
Layer chips
One per GIF on the layer (its pad key). Click to select · drag to another layer or to reorder — the leftmost is drawn on top; a bar shows where it will land · remove: × on hover, or Ctrl+click (with Ctrl held the chips turn red)
Ctrl+click
Deletes what you click (it turns red with a ✕ while Ctrl is held; ⌘ on a Mac): a pad, a scene (its name stays), a layer chip, an effect preset, a GIF in the pool (one that's on pads needs a held press: a bar sweeps across, then it leaves the pool and every pad it was on). Effects and transition presets get a red outline: Ctrl+click puts them back to their defaults. Pads and scenes come back with Ctrl+Z

GIF pool

Every GIF of this set, decoded once. Click one to put it on the next empty pad of this scene, or drag it onto a pad (replaces what's there; Ctrl+Z undoes). Each scene's pads have their own settings. Drop files or links here to add them without using a pad. Drag the title to move this window.

Sets

Sets are kept in this browser; GIF files are stored once and shared between sets. A .vjif file holds a set plus its GIFs (it's a ZIP) — use it to back up or move a set. Drop a .vjif anywhere on VJif to import it. Loading keeps the current set playing until the new one is decoded, then swaps on the next beat or bar if Snap is set.
