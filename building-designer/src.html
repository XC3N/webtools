Sprawler Building Designer

Draw buildings, the hotel and parks on the game's grid, with its tiles and colours. A cell has layers: a tile (which can face a direction), half-block pieces on top of it, and a walkable status. Designs are saved here for everyone on the project; Claude reads them to work out the rules mapgen uses to make more.
