Checks

    Keys

    P pencil · V select · M mirror · 1 2 3 tiles / pieces / status

    Pencil: click or drag to paint · Shift+click draws a straight line from the last cell you painted · Shift+right click picks the tile, piece or status under the pointer.

    Select: click a cell to see its layers; Q E turn the selected tile (or its pieces, in Pieces). With the pencil, Q E turn the brush.

    Right click: second tile in Tiles; clears the layer in Pieces and Status · Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+S save
